# Experimental visual grammar

Mappings register source field, physical domain, transformation, visual channel and representation separately. Initial channels are scalar hue, precipitation opacity, and wind filament length. Fixed physical domains prevent colors from changing meaning as time advances. Color interpolation uses linear-light sRGB.

All encodings are experiments. Scale categories are planetary, synoptic, regional and local; scale affects detail without changing altitude or physical units. Column, transect, volume and uncertainty channels are future work. No confidence score is inferred from forecast age.
